    Workshop management¶

    The area is available in Pro and Enterprise. The workspace starts empty after login. Open an overview in the left navigation; Open or a double-click opens a record in its own detail tab. Opening it again focuses the existing tab. Search and filters belong to the overview. Record actions, positions, documents and history belong to the detail workspace.

    Master data and preparation¶

    Reuse existing vehicles, personnel and suppliers. Create commercial customers, units, explicitly classified tax codes, articles, warehouses, storage locations and price groups. Device tenants are not invoice customers. Assign a customer to a vehicle where appropriate. Own vehicles can have a cost category for internal workshop costs.

    Maintain supplier purchase prices and price-group sales prices with validity periods. Personnel have separate cost and sales rates. Units distinguish count, time and other quantities. Time factors explicitly refer to hours; labour units require their own approved factor. Quantities and personnel prices are converted only between compatible units. Apply price list uses the rule valid on the service or purchase-order date and displays the calculated result. Disable it for an explicit manual price; text changes retain existing price provenance. A missing sales price requires a decision. Free service requires a reason and explicit confirmation before finalization. Referenced master records are archived.

    Orders and quotations¶

    A vehicle order proposes the vehicle's current customer. A customer order requires a commercial customer; a vehicle is optional. An alternative invoice customer is selected explicitly and audited. Changing a vehicle's customer does not silently change existing orders. Rebilling displays any difference and requires explicit confirmation of the current mapping.

    Master articles, free articles, personnel effort and labour texts are separate position types. Earmarked articles and applied templates are planned demand. They do not change stock. Conversion to actual material checks availability; partial quantities require an explicit choice. Actual material consumption posts a stock issue. Quantity changes post only their difference; price and text changes do not post quantities. An actual return is a separate operation.

    Keep planned and performed effort separate. An order can be started, completed and reopened before binding invoicing. Completion posts internal costs for an own vehicle from actual inventory values and cost rates. Repeated completion posts only a changed cost amount.

    Quotations support the same four position types and templates. A draft is editable. Issue freezes the version including customer, prices, taxes and document language. New version creates a separate draft referring to the original. Open document displays the PDF. Conversion copies the header and all positions as planned, without stock issues or performed hours. Repetition does not create another order. Conversion opens customer-order details when no vehicle is assigned and vehicle-order details when a vehicle is assigned. Opening or double-clicking a customer order retains its customer overview; the detail tab and navigation agree, and reopening creates no duplicate. Already open order overviews refresh automatically after conversion.

    Inventory and purchasing¶

    Stock is derived from an immutable movement journal. Negative available stock is prevented. Purchases, issues, returns, count differences, transfers and approved openings retain their source, business date and actual posting timestamp. Purchase drafts are editable; released orders support partial receipts. A reasoned receipt return references the original receipt and issues its current inventory value. Overdelivery and closing an outstanding quantity require an explicit reason.

    Create an inventory count document with a UTC cutoff and warehouse. It freezes the cutoff stock for each position. Enter every counted quantity and explicitly approve the document. The counted difference is applied exactly once to current stock; intervening movements remain intact. Approved documents are immutable. The cutoff report uses actual UTC posting timestamps and recorded book values; the business document date does not shift that history.

    The default valuation is the moving average per article and warehouse. Global article valuation and fixed prices are configured through a documented valuation change. Historical postings are not revalued. Purchase proposals consider minimum stock, planned demand, stock and confirmed outstanding supply; minimum purchase quantities and package sizes apply. Indicators disclose their period and missing history.

    Outgoing invoices and corrections¶

    Invoicing supports manual invoices and completed orders for one invoice customer. Previously consumed order material is not issued again. Direct sales are issued exactly once on finalization. Administration provides separate annual number ranges for invoices and corrections; reserved numbers are never reused. Drafts have no final invoice number.

    Finalization freezes reviewed data, reserves a number, generates and validates XML or hybrid PDF and seals the originals in the archive. Errors prevent issuance. Interrupted finalization resumes with the same finalization ID. A reasoned abort retains the number evidence; already sealed originals are completed.

    Issued invoices cannot be edited or deleted. Full cancellation and supported partial corrections create new documents referring to the original. Invoice correction is not a material return. Old-part and disbursement rules require versioned examples approved by a tax specialist. Unapproved special treatments are blocked.

    DATEV, CSV/ZIP and REST use the same frozen handover data. Retries preserve export ID and package. An uncertain transmission status requires clarification; without confirmed receiver idempotency the system does not resend blindly. The complete auditor export additionally includes originals, field descriptions, relationships, rule versions and audit evidence. Create auditor export in the transfer overview works without a selected or existing outgoing invoice. DATEV includes debtor master data alongside the booking batch; Finalization belongs to the approved export configuration.

    Archive status and external backup status are separate. Successful offline finalization does not confirm external backup. Follow the operating procedures and architecture and release gates.

    Retention¶

    Open Retention under Invoicing. The overview filters document classes and due reviews. Open a record to extend its deadline, apply or release a legal hold, or review an individual case. Each change requires a reason and the current record version. Linked documents share the longest deadline. Releasing a hold does not change the minimum deadline. Review reports remain traceable and perform no physical deletion; that operational action is locked.
